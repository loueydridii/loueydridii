## 👋 About Me

I'm a final-year Business Administration student at **Tunis Business School**, majoring in Information Technology with a minor in Business Analytics.

My interests sit at the intersection of technology, data, and business. I enjoy breaking down complex problems, understanding the information behind them, and building systems that turn ideas into practical solutions.

- 🤖 **AI & Automation:** Integrating LLMs, experimenting with AI agents, and building intelligent workflows.
- 📊 **Data & Analytics:** Processing data, exploring patterns, applying analytical methods, and building BI dashboards.
- 💻 **Software Development:** Building web applications and connecting services, APIs, databases, and interfaces.
- 🧩 **Systems Thinking:** Understanding the problem before choosing the technology, and connecting technical solutions to real needs.
- 🎨 **Creative Technology:** Combining design, visual communication, and technical implementation.

I like exploring beyond the boundaries of a single discipline. My background combines business, analytics, software, and creative work, giving me different perspectives when approaching a problem.
